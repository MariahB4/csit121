Why do we even need WOFF?

The main reason WOFF matters so much is for performance and speed. Since WOFF 2.0 compresses files so well, it saves a lot of bandwidth for the person visiting the site. This is a huge deal for people on mobile phones or anyone with a terrible internet connection. If fonts took forever to load, people would probably just leave the site before they even read anything. WOFF makes sure the text shows up as fast as possible.

Another big part of its importance is about creative freedom for designers. Back in the day, you had to use "web-safe" fonts, which meant every site looked pretty much the same. Now, because of this standard, a brand can use its own custom font and be sure it looks the same on a Mac, a PC, or a phone. It gives the internet a lot more personality and helps different companies stand out from each other visually.

Lastly, it's really important for the people who actually make the fonts. WOFF allows for metadata to be stored inside the file, which can include licensing info and creator details. Even though it's not a "lock" that stops people from ever stealing a font, it creates a professional system for using them legally. It’s basically a win-win because designers get to use cool fonts and the creators get their work protected and recognized properly.
